About

milanocity.lol is a public leaderboard for Italian startups. Anyone can list their site, app, or profile and pay in euros to move up. There's no subscription and no reserved spot — if someone pays more than you, they take your rank. Payments are real and processed by Stripe; the leaderboard itself is read straight from confirmed Stripe payments, so there's no separate database to fake.

Rules

  • Minimum bid is €5, charged via Stripe Checkout.
  • To reach #1 you must bid more than the current #1.
  • Lower bids still claim whatever rank they can outrank.
  • Re-enter the same URL/handle to top up your own bid — amounts accumulate.
  • Be reasonable — this is a community leaderboard, not a real marketplace.
