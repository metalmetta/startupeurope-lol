About

outbid.lol is a public leaderboard. Anyone can list their site, app, or profile and pay to move up. There's no subscription and no reserved spot — if someone pays more than you, they take your rank. This is a fan-made clone built for fun; it stores everything locally in your browser and doesn't process real payments.

Rules

  • Minimum bid is $5.
  • To reach #1 you must bid more than the current #1.
  • Lower bids still claim whatever rank they can outrank.
  • Re-enter the same URL/handle to top up your own bid.
  • Be reasonable — this is a demo, not a real marketplace.
